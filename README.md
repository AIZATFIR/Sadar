## 🌟 Overview

**Sadar** is designed around a single fundamental question:
> **"Did I live today in a way I can be proud of?"**

Rather than obsessing over fragile numerical streaks, Sadar turns meaningful daily actions into visible, accumulated evidence of a life lived with awareness and intentional repetition.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Multi-Type Habits**:
  - **Timed (YPT Focus Mode)**: Fullscreen focus timer with lockscreen support and allowed-apps whitelist.
  - **Count Mode**: Simple numeric tracking (e.g. 1 course, 20x pushups).
  - **Bodybuilding / Flexible Progression**: Dynamic daily progression that adapts automatically.
  - **Hybrid Mode**: Combined time & count tracking.
- 📅 **7-Day Horizontal Timeline**: 1-tap completion recording (<5 seconds) across a calm horizontal timeline.
- 🔁 **What I Repeat (Repetisi)**: Visual aggregations of what habits you consistently practice.
- 💡 **Awareness & Reflections**: End-of-day introspection ("Proud of Today" & emotional state tracking).
- 🔒 **Local-First & Anti-Brick Protection**: Safe flags prevent locking users out while maintaining distraction-free focus.
- ⏱️ **Focus Clock Integration**: Connects with Focus Clock events for seamless schedule time-blocking.
